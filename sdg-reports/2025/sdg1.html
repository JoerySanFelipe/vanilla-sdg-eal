Institutional Framework for Poverty Alleviation

Urdaneta City University (UCU) recognizes that poverty is a multidimensional challenge that extends far beyond a simple lack of financial resources. It encompasses systemic barriers to opportunity, educational disenfranchisement, and limited access to critical community infrastructure. In alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 1, our institution has deployed a mathematically rigorous, evidence-based approach to dismantling these barriers.

Our core philosophy dictates that a university must serve as an economic engine for its surrounding municipalities. By aggressively subsidizing tuition for students from bottom-quartile income brackets and systematically restructuring our outreach programs, we have transformed our External Office and Linkages division into a command center for socio-economic mobility.

Community Empowerment Strategies

To ensure our impact transcends the campus boundaries, the External Office and Linkages has integrated deep-rooted community action plans. These are not merely charitable exercises; they are sustainable capacity-building frameworks. By partnering directly with the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), we have synchronized our student-led extension programs with national anti-poverty directives.

The most prominent example of this strategic alignment is our institutional support and technical integration with the KALAHI-CIDSS initiative. Through this partnership, UCU engineering and sociology departments provided crucial data mapping and infrastructure planning to ensure localized poverty reduction funds were deployed with maximum efficiency.

Livelihood Incubation & Micro-Enterprise

Eradicating poverty requires transitioning vulnerable populations from dependence to self-sufficiency. The UCU College of Business and Accountancy, operating through our External Office, established the Livelihood Incubation Hub in late 2021. This facility provides direct seed capital, accounting mentorship, and market access strategies to families residing in the lowest socio-economic deciles.

Earn-While-You-Learn Initiative

True commitment to eradicating poverty starts from within the campus infrastructure. UCU recognizes that financial barriers are the primary cause of student attrition. To combat this, the university established the Earn-While-You-Learn (EWYL) initiative, a formalized student employment matrix designed to provide living wages and immediate financial relief to marginalized enrollees.
