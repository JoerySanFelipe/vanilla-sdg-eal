Urdaneta City University actively engages students in community service initiatives that promote environmental stewardship, social responsibility, public education, and sustainable community development. Through extension programs and institutional partnerships, students apply their academic knowledge and skills in addressing local sustainability challenges while fostering civic engagement and volunteerism. These projects are conducted regularly throughout the academic year and involve collaboration with schools, local government units, community organizations, and other stakeholders.

Some of the university's sustainability-related community service projects include:

Project Name Participants Project Duration Project Area Description
Nuclear Energy and Safety Awareness Seminar 300 Yearly SI An annual educational seminar that raises awareness among students and community members on the safe, peaceful, and sustainable use of nuclear energy. The program promotes scientific literacy, energy sustainability, radiation safety, and environmental responsibility through lectures and interactive discussions.
Project WOW 100 Quarterly EC A quarterly community outreach initiative that encourages environmental conservation through waste management campaigns, clean-up drives, tree planting, and environmental awareness activities. The project strengthens students' commitment to ecological sustainability and responsible environmental practices.
Badang Brigada Eskwela and Panagtaripato Kadagiti Agtutubo More than 20 schools Yearly TR An annual extension program that mobilizes students to assist public schools through volunteer work, campus improvement, educational support, and youth development activities. The project promotes quality education, community collaboration, and social sustainability by creating safe and inclusive learning environments.

These initiatives demonstrate Urdaneta City University's commitment to integrating sustainability into community engagement by providing students with meaningful opportunities to contribute to environmental protection, educational development, and community well-being while supporting the achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
