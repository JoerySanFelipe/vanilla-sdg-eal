Number of study program related to sustainability offered 38 study programs

Urdaneta City University integrates sustainability across its academic programs by embedding the principles of environmental stewardship, social responsibility, public health, inclusive education, ethical governance, sustainable infrastructure, innovation, responsible business practices, community engagement, and sustainable economic development into its curricula. These sustainability dimensions are reflected in program educational objectives, learning outcomes, instruction, research, extension services, industry immersion, and community-based learning initiatives.

The University currently offers thirty-eight (38) sustainability-related study programs spanning graduate, professional, and undergraduate education. These include programs in education, health sciences, pharmacy, engineering, architecture, information technology, hospitality and tourism management, business and accountancy, communication, social work, criminology, law, and public administration. Each program contributes to one or more dimensions of sustainable development by preparing graduates to address contemporary environmental, social, economic, technological, governance, and public health challenges.

Through these academic offerings, Urdaneta City University demonstrates its strong institutional commitment to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by producing competent, ethical, innovative, and socially responsible professionals capable of contributing to sustainable local, national, and global development.
