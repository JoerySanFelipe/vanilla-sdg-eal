The Narrative Report on Faculty Research Publications (2022–2024) provides a compelling overview of the university’s research productivity, reflecting its ongoing commitment to knowledge creation and sustainable academic development. Over the three-year period, the report captures both progress and challenges in translating research efforts into published outputs.

In 2022, the university recorded 7 publications from 31 faculty researchers, achieving a notable 22.6% publication rate. This promising start highlights a strong foundation for scholarly engagement. However, in 2023, despite a significant increase in the number of researchers to 139, no publications were recorded, indicating a critical gap between research participation and actual dissemination of outputs. By 2024, with an expanded pool of 232 researchers, the institution produced 6 publications, equivalent to 2.6%, showing early signs of recovery but also emphasizing the need for stronger support systems to improve productivity.

These trends suggest that while faculty involvement in research is steadily growing, there remains a need to strengthen mechanisms that support publication, collaboration, and knowledge sharing. Enhancing research capability, providing targeted incentives, fostering interdisciplinary partnerships, and promoting a culture of sustainability-driven inquiry can significantly improve publication outcomes.

Moving forward, the university is well-positioned to transform these insights into strategic actions—ensuring that research efforts not only increase in quantity but also contribute meaningfully to global knowledge, societal development, and long-term institutional impact.
