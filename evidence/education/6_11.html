Urdaneta City University demonstrates a strong and sustained institutional commitment to sustainability through a wide range of extension, academic, research, and community engagement initiatives. These efforts are designed to advance environmental protection, social responsibility, economic resilience, and cultural preservation, while strengthening the university’s contribution to sustainable development at both local and national levels.

Over the past three academic years, the university has consistently implemented sustainability-related programs in collaboration with its academic units, student organizations, external partners, and community stakeholders. These initiatives include conferences, seminars, workshops, training programs, awareness campaigns, and outreach activities that collectively aim to deepen understanding of sustainability issues, build relevant competencies, and encourage active participation in addressing environmental and societal challenges.

Based on consolidated institutional records, Urdaneta City University records an average of 120 sustainability-related events per year over the last three years. This sustained level of activity reflects the university’s deliberate and organized effort to integrate sustainability into its core functions of instruction, research, extension, and community engagement.

These events commonly include environmental awareness campaigns such as clean-up drives and tree planting activities, sustainability-focused seminars and webinars, disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation trainings, community outreach and extension programs, health and sanitation initiatives, as well as academic conferences and forums on sustainable development.

Overall, the university’s consistent and coordinated implementation of these activities underscores its commitment to embedding sustainability into institutional culture and strengthening its role as a proactive partner in advancing sustainable development goals within the broader community.
