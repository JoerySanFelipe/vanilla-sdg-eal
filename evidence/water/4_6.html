Urdaneta City University (UCU) has established a campus-wide rainwater harvesting system as a core component of its sustainable water management strategy, directly supporting the Water and Infrastructure indicators of the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings. Every building on campus is equipped with an integrated and efficient drainage network designed to capture rainwater from rooftops and channel it into designated storage facilities. This system ensures the optimal collection and utilization of rainwater, reducing reliance on potable water sources and promoting efficient resource use.

The harvested rainwater is carefully managed and distributed for both environmental and operational purposes. A portion of the collected water is directed to nearby ponds and canal systems, contributing to natural water recharge and supporting the surrounding ecosystem. This practice also helps manage excess rainfall, reducing surface runoff and mitigating the risk of flooding within and around the campus. By integrating rainwater into the natural water cycle, the university promotes ecological balance and sustainable water flow management.

In alignment with GreenMetric’s emphasis on efficient water use, several campus facilities have been specifically designated for the reuse of harvested rainwater. One of its primary applications is in toilet flushing systems, which significantly reduces the consumption of potable water across the university. This substitution of alternative water sources for non-potable uses represents a key strategy in minimizing water waste and improving overall water-use efficiency.

In addition, rainwater is utilized for landscape irrigation and the maintenance of green spaces across the campus. This ensures that gardens, lawns, and planted areas remain well-maintained throughout the year without heavily relying on municipal water supply. The consistent use of rainwater for irrigation supports sustainable landscaping practices and contributes to the preservation of the university’s green environment.

The integration of this system reflects UCU’s commitment to sustainable infrastructure and resource conservation, key elements of the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings. By prioritizing the use of renewable water sources and reducing dependence on potable water, the university demonstrates responsible water management and long-term environmental planning.

Beyond its functional benefits, the rainwater harvesting program enhances the campus’s resilience to climate-related challenges, such as flooding and water scarcity. It ensures a sustainable water supply for non-potable uses while also contributing to environmental protection. Furthermore, the availability of water for vegetation supports biodiversity and maintains the aesthetic quality of the campus, creating a greener, healthier, and more inviting environment for students, faculty, staff, and visitors.

Water Pollution Control Program & Wastewater Management
Description

The Water Pollution Control Program at Urdaneta City University (UCU) has been successfully implemented as a key component of the university’s environmental sustainability strategy, contributing to the protection and improvement of water quality within and around the campus. Aligned with the Water and Environmental Management indicators of the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, the program focuses on pollution prevention, systematic water quality monitoring, community awareness, and institutional accountability.

A central feature of the program is the implementation of Best Management Practices (BMPs) for waste handling and disposal. The university has established strict protocols for managing hazardous and non-hazardous waste, particularly in laboratories and high-risk areas. Proper segregation, storage, and disposal procedures are strictly enforced, and designated waste collection and disposal areas have been established to prevent improper discharge of contaminants. These measures significantly reduce the risk of pollutants entering nearby water bodies through runoff, thereby protecting both surface and groundwater quality.

To ensure continuous assessment and data-driven management, UCU has developed a comprehensive water quality monitoring system. Regular testing is conducted on campus water bodies, including ponds, canals, and other aquatic environments. Key water quality parameters are measured and analyzed to identify potential sources of contamination and assess the effectiveness of implemented control measures. The data gathered are systematically recorded and used to guide policy decisions, improve pollution control strategies, and support evidence-based environmental management—an approach that aligns with GreenMetric’s emphasis on monitoring and reporting.

The program also places strong emphasis on education, awareness, and behavioral change, which are essential components of sustainable water management under the GreenMetric framework. Workshops, seminars, and awareness campaigns are regularly conducted to educate the university community about the causes and impacts of water pollution, as well as preventive practices. These initiatives encourage responsible behavior, such as proper waste disposal, reduced use of harmful chemicals, and adherence to environmental guidelines. Sustainable landscaping practices are likewise promoted, including the use of eco-friendly fertilizers and reduced chemical inputs, to minimize the risk of runoff and contamination.

Community engagement plays a vital role in strengthening the program’s impact. Students, faculty, and staff actively participate in environmental activities such as waterway cleanup drives, monitoring exercises, and advocacy campaigns. The establishment of a Water Quality Task Force, composed of representatives from various sectors of the university, further enhances coordination and ensures sustained implementation of pollution control measures. This collaborative approach fosters a strong sense of environmental stewardship and shared responsibility among stakeholders.

The program is further reinforced through partnerships with local government units, environmental agencies, and research institutions, which provide technical expertise, additional resources, and opportunities for collaborative research. These partnerships have led to joint studies on local water quality and pollution trends, contributing to a deeper understanding of environmental challenges and more effective intervention strategies. Such collaborations align with GreenMetric’s emphasis on research, external engagement, and community impact.

Implementation of the program followed a structured and strategic approach, beginning with a comprehensive assessment of water quality and pollution sources. This was followed by the establishment of preventive measures, continuous monitoring, and sustained educational campaigns. Clear roles and responsibilities were assigned, with a Program Coordinator overseeing overall implementation and specialized teams managing monitoring, communication, and environmental safety functions. This governance structure ensures efficient coordination and accountability across all program components.

The program is supported by funding from university allocations, grants, and external partnerships, which have enabled the acquisition of monitoring equipment, development of educational materials, and organization of community-based environmental activities. Evaluation metrics indicate measurable improvements in water quality, increased participation from the university community, and a significant reduction in identified pollution sources. These outcomes demonstrate the program’s effectiveness in achieving its environmental objectives.
Wastewater Treatment

The Wastewater Treatment Program at Urdaneta City University (UCU) has been successfully implemented as a critical component of the university’s sustainable water management system, directly contributing to the Water and Environmental Management indicators of the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings. The program is designed to minimize environmental impact, enhance resource recovery, ensure regulatory compliance, and promote a culture of sustainability across the university community.

A key feature of the program is the establishment of a modern wastewater treatment facility equipped with advanced and efficient treatment technologies. Wastewater generated from university buildings, laboratories, and other facilities undergoes a multi-stage treatment process, which may include activated sludge systems, biofiltration, and membrane-based filtration technologies. These processes effectively remove contaminants, reduce biological and chemical pollutants, and produce high-quality effluent that meets environmental standards. The facility ensures that wastewater is treated safely before being released or reused, thereby preventing pollution of nearby water bodies.

In alignment with GreenMetric’s emphasis on water efficiency and resource optimization, UCU has integrated resource recovery and reuse strategies into its wastewater management system. Treated wastewater is reused for non-potable applications such as landscape irrigation, toilet flushing, and cleaning operations. This significantly reduces the university’s demand for potable water and supports sustainable water consumption practices. Additionally, the program explores innovative recovery options, including nutrient extraction for use as fertilizers and the potential production of biogas for renewable energy, further enhancing the circular economy approach within the campus.

To ensure the effectiveness and safety of the treatment system, UCU has established a rigorous monitoring and evaluation framework. Regular testing of key water quality parameters is conducted to assess the efficiency of the treatment process and ensure compliance with environmental regulations and national water quality standards. Continuous monitoring allows the university to detect any deviations early, make necessary adjustments, and maintain consistent treatment performance. Data collected from these activities are systematically recorded and used for reporting, evaluation, and continuous improvement—an important requirement under the UI GreenMetric framework.

Education and awareness are also integral to the success of the program. The university conducts workshops, seminars, and training sessions to educate students, faculty, and staff on wastewater treatment processes, water pollution prevention, and the importance of proper water management. Informational materials, including posters and digital campaigns, further reinforce awareness and promote responsible behavior. These initiatives help build a knowledgeable and environmentally conscious university community, aligning with GreenMetric’s focus on education and capacity building.

Community engagement is actively promoted through the involvement of students and faculty in the operation, observation, and monitoring of the wastewater treatment facility. This hands-on participation provides practical learning opportunities while strengthening environmental stewardship within the academic community. Partnerships with local government units, environmental agencies, and research institutions further enhance the program by providing technical expertise, supporting research initiatives, and encouraging collaboration on sustainable water management solutions.

The program follows a structured and strategic implementation framework. Initial stages included a comprehensive assessment of campus wastewater generation and management practices, followed by the planning, design, and operationalization of the treatment facility. With the facility now fully operational, the program continues to implement treatment, monitoring, and resource recovery processes while expanding its educational and research components. Clear roles and responsibilities have been established, with a Program Coordinator overseeing overall implementation and specialized teams managing operations, monitoring, education, and research activities.
Guideline Standards

The Wastewater Treatment Program at Urdaneta City University (UCU) adheres to comprehensive technical, operational, and environmental guidelines to ensure effective wastewater management and full compliance with local and national regulations. These standards are aligned with the sustainability and environmental management criteria of the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, particularly under water management, infrastructure, and environmental policy.

The program follows established protocols for the design, operation, and maintenance of its wastewater treatment facility, ensuring that all processes meet or exceed regulatory requirements set by environmental authorities. The system incorporates Best Management Practices (BMPs) for the handling, storage, and disposal of hazardous materials, especially from laboratories and research facilities, to prevent contamination and ensure safe wastewater discharge.

Strict adherence to effluent quality standards is enforced, with treated water required to meet prescribed limits for parameters such as biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), total suspended solids (TSS), pH levels, nutrient content, and microbial indicators. These standards ensure that any discharged or reused water is safe for the environment and compliant with environmental protection regulations.

To maintain operational efficiency and compliance, the university conducts regular capacity building and training programs for personnel, including facility operators, maintenance staff, and student volunteers. These training sessions focus on proper system operation, safety protocols, environmental compliance, and sustainable resource recovery practices. By building technical competence and awareness, UCU ensures that all stakeholders are actively engaged in maintaining high operational standards.

The program also integrates sustainable resource recovery principles, promoting the reuse of treated wastewater for non-potable applications such as irrigation, cleaning, and sanitation. In addition, it explores opportunities for nutrient recovery and energy generation, such as biogas production, supporting circular economy practices and reducing overall environmental impact. These initiatives reflect UCU’s commitment to maximizing resource efficiency and minimizing waste, in line with GreenMetric sustainability objectives.
Monitoring and Evaluation

A comprehensive monitoring and evaluation (M&E) system is a core component of the Wastewater Treatment Program, ensuring continuous assessment of system performance and environmental compliance. The program conducts regular laboratory testing of key water quality parameters, including biochemical oxygen demand (BOD), total suspended solids (TSS), nutrient concentrations (such as nitrogen and phosphorus), pH levels, and pathogen counts. These indicators are critical in evaluating the effectiveness of the treatment processes and ensuring that effluent quality meets or exceeds regulatory standards.

Data collected from monitoring activities are systematically recorded, analyzed, and interpreted to identify trends, detect potential issues, and guide operational improvements. This data-driven approach allows the university to continuously refine its treatment processes, optimize system performance, and ensure long-term sustainability—key aspects emphasized in the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings.

The program also includes periodic performance reviews of both operational efficiency and resource recovery outcomes. These evaluations assess treatment effectiveness, system reliability, and the extent to which treated wastewater and recovered resources are being utilized within campus operations. Findings from these reviews are used to inform decision-making, improve system design, and enhance overall program effectiveness.

Community involvement is actively integrated into the monitoring process. Students and faculty are encouraged to participate in monitoring activities, field observations, and environmental assessments, fostering a culture of environmental stewardship and experiential learning. This participatory approach not only enhances transparency but also strengthens awareness and engagement in sustainable water management practices.

To ensure accountability and transparency, UCU publishes annual reports documenting the program’s performance, achievements, challenges, and future plans. These reports serve as official records of compliance and progress, providing stakeholders with clear and accessible information on the program’s environmental impact. The documentation and reporting process aligns with GreenMetric’s emphasis on transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement in sustainability performance.
