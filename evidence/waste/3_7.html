Marawi/STP: Transforming Organic Waste into Sustainability

At the core of Urdaneta City University’s sustainability framework is the Marawi/STP (Sewage Treatment Plant), a key facility that supports the University’s integrated approach to organic waste treatment and environmental management. Aligned with the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, particularly under the Waste (WS) and Setting & Infrastructure (SI) indicators, the Marawi/STP demonstrates UCU’s commitment to sustainable campus operations and resource recovery systems.

The Marawi/STP functions as an innovative solution for managing organic waste and wastewater by converting biodegradable materials into reusable and environmentally beneficial outputs. Through natural and engineered treatment processes, the facility transforms organic waste into nutrient-rich by-products that can be utilized to enhance soil quality, support landscaping initiatives, and sustain campus greening programs. This closed-loop system reflects the principles of a circular economy, where waste is minimized and resources are continuously reused.

Beyond its technical function, the Marawi/STP plays a vital role in reducing the University’s environmental impact. By treating organic waste on-site, the facility helps minimize landfill dependence, reduce pollution, and mitigate greenhouse gas emissions. It also contributes to improved water quality management by ensuring that treated water meets environmental standards before reuse or discharge.
