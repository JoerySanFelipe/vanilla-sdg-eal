Urdaneta City University (UCU) demonstrates a strong institutional commitment to sustainability and responsible waste management through its recent assessment of organic waste generation, in alignment with the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, particularly under the Waste (WS) and Setting & Infrastructure (SI) categories. The systematic collection and analysis of data provide critical insights into the composition and volume of biodegradable waste, enabling the University to better understand and manage its environmental footprint.

Based on the assessment, UCU generated a total of 11.3 tons of organic waste, consisting entirely of leaf waste. This indicates that the University’s landscaped areas, green spaces, and routine grounds maintenance activities are the primary sources of organic waste, with seasonal variations—such as leaf shedding—significantly influencing waste volume. The data highlights the importance of integrating sustainable landscape management practices into the institution’s overall waste management strategy.

Notably, the dataset records no food waste generation, reflecting the effectiveness of UCU’s food service operations and waste minimization strategies. This outcome suggests the successful implementation of measures such as portion control, efficient food preparation, and responsible consumption among students and staff. These practices not only reduce waste at the source but also align with sustainability principles and institutional policies promoting responsible resource use.

Given the considerable volume of leaf waste, UCU has a strategic opportunity to strengthen its environmental initiatives through the expansion of composting systems. By converting organic waste into nutrient-rich compost, the University can:

  • • Minimize waste sent to landfills and reduce associated environmental impacts;
  • • Support campus landscaping, urban greening, and agricultural projects;
  • • Promote a circular waste management approach by reintegrating organic materials into productive use.
