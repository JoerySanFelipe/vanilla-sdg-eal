The data reflects institutional efforts to minimize waste generation, improve resource efficiency, and strengthen sustainable waste management practices at the source level.

Plastic Waste: In the previous year, the institution generated a total of 161 tons of plastic waste, of which 147 tons were successfully reduced, resulting in a net reduction of 14 tons. In the current year, a more decentralized monitoring approach shows that Source A generated 81 tons with a reduction of 6 tons, while Source B generated 80 tons with a reduction of 8 tons. This source-based breakdown highlights improved tracking and accountability mechanisms, supporting targeted interventions such as plastic use minimization, recycling initiatives, and awareness campaigns. These efforts contribute directly to reducing single-use plastics, a key component of GreenMetric’s waste reduction goals.

Paper Waste: For paper waste, the previous year recorded 180 tons of production, with 160 tons reduced, achieving a reduction of 16 tons. In the current year, Source A produced 70 tons with a reduction of 10 tons, while Source B produced 100 tons with a reduction of 5 tons, complemented by smaller contributions from other units. The data reflects strengthened paper conservation strategies, including digitalization initiatives, double-sided printing policies, and increased reuse and recycling practices. These measures align with sustainable resource consumption and institutional efficiency targets.

Overall Assessment: The year-on-year comparison demonstrates a consistent and measurable decline in both plastic and paper waste generation, indicating the effectiveness of ongoing sustainability programs. The integration of source-level data enhances strategic planning by identifying high-impact areas for further intervention.

Overall, the data underscores the institution’s commitment to continuous improvement in waste management, contributing to a more resource-efficient, environmentally responsible, and GreenMetric-compliant campus.
