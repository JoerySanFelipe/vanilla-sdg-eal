1. Eco-Craft: Turning Plastic into Possibilities

Eco-Craft: Turning Plastic into Possibilities is a sustainability-driven, hands-on workshop designed to advance institutional environmental initiatives in alignment with the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, particularly under the Waste (WS) and Education & Research (ED) categories. The program promotes innovative waste management practices by transforming discarded plastic water bottles into functional products and creative artworks, demonstrating practical approaches to waste reduction and material recovery.

Through guided activities, participants gain a deeper understanding of plastic waste challenges, circular economy principles, and the environmental impacts of improper disposal. The workshop integrates sustainability education by encouraging critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving—key competencies emphasized in GreenMetric’s framework for environmental education.

Eco-Craft also supports institutional goals in minimizing single-use plastics and enhancing campus-based sustainability engagement. By equipping participants with upcycling skills and environmental awareness, the program contributes to reducing waste generation while fostering a culture of sustainability that extends beyond the campus into households and local communities.

Ultimately, Eco-Craft serves as a replicable model for integrating sustainability into co-curricular activities, strengthening the university’s commitment to environmental stewardship, innovation, and GreenMetric compliance.

2. It’s Segregation Week

It’s Segregation Week is a comprehensive environmental awareness campaign that reinforces proper waste management practices in alignment with the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings, particularly addressing the Waste (WS), Setting & Infrastructure (SI), and Education & Research (ED) indicators. The initiative is designed to institutionalize effective waste segregation systems and promote responsible consumption behaviors across the campus and surrounding community.

The program features interactive learning sessions, waste-sorting demonstrations, campus-wide segregation challenges, and community engagement activities that emphasize the importance of separating biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and hazardous wastes. Participants are guided on how proper segregation contributes to reducing landfill waste, improving recycling efficiency, and supporting resource conservation.

Segregation Week also highlights the university’s commitment to sustainable infrastructure by encouraging the proper use of designated waste bins and reinforcing existing campus waste management policies. Educational components of the program integrate sustainability concepts into everyday practices, empowering participants to become active contributors to environmental protection. By fostering collective action and behavioral change, It’s Segregation Week strengthens environmental responsibility, enhances campus sustainability performance, and supports the university’s continuous improvement in GreenMetric rankings. The initiative ultimately contributes to building a cleaner, more resource-efficient, and environmentally resilient academic community.
