RA 10173
Urdaneta City University complies with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), providing GDPR-equivalent data protection safeguards.

Urdaneta City University Data Protection and Privacy Policy provides GDPR-equivalent compliance under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Data Protection and Privacy Policy Mapping
Policy Component Description
Policy Title Data Protection and Privacy Policy
Legal Basis Republic Act No. 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012 and Implementing Rules and Regulations
Policy Objective To protect the personal, sensitive, and confidential information of students, employees, stakeholders, and partners while ensuring lawful, transparent, and secure data processing practices.
Coverage Applies to all offices, employees, faculty, students, researchers, partners, and third-party service providers of the University handling personal data.
Data Collection Principles Personal data shall be collected fairly, lawfully, and only for legitimate institutional purposes such as enrollment, employment, research, extension services, and digital transactions.
Data Subject Rights Individuals have the right to be informed, object, access, correct, erase/block, data portability, damages, and file complaints pursuant to the Data Privacy Act.
AI and Digital Technologies Compliance AI systems, cloud platforms, IoT devices, biometrics, and digital monitoring tools shall comply with privacy-by-design principles and institutional cybersecurity protocols.
Security Measures The University shall implement organizational, physical, and technical safeguards including:
  • password protection
  • multi-factor authentication
  • encrypted databases
  • secured servers
  • restricted access controls
  • regular cybersecurity audits
Data Retention and Disposal Personal information shall only be retained for the period necessary to fulfill institutional and legal purposes and shall be securely disposed afterward.
Data Sharing and Third-Party Processing Data sharing shall only occur with lawful basis, consent when required, and under formal Data Sharing Agreements (DSA).
Incident and Breach Management All data breaches shall be reported immediately to the designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) and managed according to NPC breach notification procedures.
Responsible Office ICT Office / Management Information System Office / Data Protection Officer
Monitoring and Review Annual review and compliance assessment shall be conducted to ensure alignment with NPC issuances and evolving digital technologies.
Expected Outcomes Enhanced cybersecurity, responsible digital governance, protection of stakeholder information, improved trust, and compliance with national and international privacy standards.
