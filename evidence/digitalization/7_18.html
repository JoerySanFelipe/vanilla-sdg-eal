Urdaneta City University recognizes the importance of promoting transparency, accountability, and ethical governance in all institutional operations. The university is committed to establishing a strong anti-corruption and integrity framework to ensure that all academic, administrative, and financial processes are conducted with fairness and integrity.

At present, the university's anti-corruption and integrity system is currently in the planning stage. Initial efforts are being undertaken to develop appropriate policies, mechanisms, and institutional structures that will support the implementation of a formal integrity system across the university.

These developments include the proposed formulation of:

  • Institutional policies on ethical conduct and anti-corruption practices;
  • Reporting and grievance mechanisms for integrity-related concerns;
  • Strengthening of internal controls and compliance procedures;
  • Capacity-building programs on ethics, accountability, and good governance; and
  • Coordination with relevant offices to ensure alignment with national regulations and standards on public sector integrity.

While the system is not yet fully operational, the university demonstrates its commitment to institutionalizing integrity measures as part of its long-term governance and sustainability agenda.
