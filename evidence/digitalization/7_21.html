Urdaneta City University recognizes the importance of having a comprehensive Code of Ethics to guide the behavior and professional conduct of its institutional leaders, academic staff, administrative personnel, and students. Such a policy is essential in promoting integrity, accountability, professionalism, and respect within the university community.

At present, the university's Code of Ethics is in the preparation and draft stage. The development of the document is currently underway as part of the university's continuing efforts to strengthen institutional governance and ensure alignment with ethical standards in higher education.

The draft Code of Ethics is envisioned to cover the following key areas:

  • Ethical standards for university leaders and decision-makers;
  • Professional conduct and responsibilities of academic staff;
  • Rules and guidelines for administrative personnel in service delivery;
  • Student behavior, academic integrity, and disciplinary standards;
  • Policies on conflict of interest, misconduct, and grievance procedures;
  • Mechanisms for enforcement, reporting, and review of ethical violations.

Once finalized and approved, the Code of Ethics will serve as an institutional framework to uphold integrity and foster a culture of accountability and ethical behavior across all sectors of the university community.
