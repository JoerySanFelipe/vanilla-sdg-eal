6 Policies
Urdaneta City University implements 6 formal policies on advanced digital technologies (AI/IoT, etc.) to support decision-making, efficiency, and service delivery.
Policies on Advanced Digital Technologies
Policy Title Objectives Key Provisions Responsible Office/Personnel Expected Outcomes
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Governance Policy To ensure the ethical, responsible, and efficient use of AI technologies in institutional operations and decision-making.
  • Use AI tools for data analytics, forecasting, report generation, and administrative support.
  • Ensure human oversight in all AI-assisted decisions.
  • Protect confidential data and privacy.
  • Conduct annual AI literacy and ethics training for employees.
 ICT Office, Planning Office, HR Office Faster decision-making, improved operational efficiency, enhanced data accuracy, and better public service delivery.
Internet of Things (IoT) Integration Policy To improve institutional efficiency, monitoring, and automation using IoT-enabled devices and systems.
  • Install IoT devices for attendance, facility monitoring, smart energy management, and security.
  • Require secure network connection and authorized device registration.
  • Conduct regular maintenance and cybersecurity checks.
  • Ensure compliance with data privacy regulations.
 ICT Office, General Services Office, Security Office Real-time monitoring, reduced operational costs, improved safety and resource management.
Digital Decision-Support System Policy To institutionalize the use of digital systems and analytics in evidence-based planning and governance.
  • Utilize digital dashboards and databases for monitoring KPIs and institutional performance.
  • Integrate analytics tools in strategic planning and budgeting.
  • Require timely updating of digital records by concerned offices.
  • Maintain backup and cybersecurity protocols.
 MIS/ICT Office, Planning Office Accurate and data-driven decisions, improved transparency, faster reporting processes.
Cloud-Based Service Delivery Policy To enhance accessibility, collaboration, and efficiency through cloud-based platforms and services.
  • Adopt cloud platforms for document sharing, communication, and collaboration.
  • Encourage paperless transactions and e-services.
  • Require secure login credentials and regular password updates.
  • Provide orientation on proper cloud usage.
 ICT Office, Records Office, All Departments Improved service accessibility, faster transactions, reduced paper consumption, and better collaboration.
Cybersecurity and Data Privacy Policy for Advanced Technologies To safeguard institutional information systems and digital technologies from cyber threats and unauthorized access.
  • Enforce strong cybersecurity measures and multi-factor authentication.
  • Conduct periodic vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity awareness programs.
  • Regulate access to AI and IoT-generated data.
  • Ensure compliance with the Data Privacy Act.
 Data Protection Officer, ICT Office Reduced cybersecurity risks, protection of sensitive information, improved stakeholder trust.
Digital Transformation and Innovation Policy To promote innovation and continuous improvement through emerging digital technologies.
  • Encourage adoption of AI, IoT, automation, and smart technologies.
  • Allocate budget for digital innovation projects.
  • Establish innovation and technology committees.
  • Conduct regular evaluation of digital initiatives.
 Administration, ICT Office, Research & Development Office Increased institutional innovation, streamlined services, modernized operations.
