The Campus Commute Connect initiative of Urdaneta City University aims to reduce the number of private vehicles on campus while promoting sustainable commuting practices. This program proposes the establishment of a comprehensive network of diesel or hybrid buses that will operate on a frequent and reliable schedule, connecting key areas within the campus and nearby residential communities. By providing accessible and convenient public transportation, the initiative encourages students, faculty, and staff to shift from private vehicles to shared mobility options.

This approach is designed to reduce traffic congestion, lower carbon emissions, and decrease parking demand within the university. It complements existing sustainability efforts by supporting more efficient transportation systems and reducing the environmental impact of daily commuting. In addition, the initiative includes the development of dedicated bike lanes and secure bicycle parking facilities, further promoting cycling as a safe, practical, and eco-friendly mode of transportation.

Through the integration of shared transport systems and cycling infrastructure, Campus Commute Connect reinforces UCU’s commitment to sustainable mobility and environmental responsibility. The program not only enhances accessibility and convenience but also fosters a culture of sustainability, encouraging the university community to adopt greener commuting habits and contribute to a cleaner, more efficient campus environment.
