Urdaneta City University (UCU) provides a regular, university-operated shuttle service that is free of charge for students, faculty, staff, and authorized visitors. The shuttle enhances campus accessibility by providing safe, convenient, and reliable transportation between key academic buildings, administrative offices, student facilities, parking areas, and other essential locations within the campus.

As part of the University’s commitment to sustainable campus mobility, the free shuttle service encourages the use of shared transportation, helping reduce dependence on private vehicles, alleviate traffic congestion, and lower greenhouse gas emissions. The service also promotes inclusive access by ensuring that members of the university community can conveniently travel across the campus regardless of distance or mobility needs.

The UCU shuttle system supports the University’s sustainability objectives under the UI GreenMetric Transportation category by fostering efficient, accessible, and environmentally responsible campus transportation while enhancing the overall campus experience.
