NO SHUTTLE SERVICE AS OF PREVIOUS DATA

Under the UI GreenMetric World University Rankings framework, if a shuttle service is not provided due to positive reason(s)—such as a compact campus footprint or the complete availability of alternative active transit pathways—the university selects the option **"shuttle use is not applicable"**.

UI GreenMetric Shuttle Services Classification

Option 3: Provided (by university or other parties) and the university contributes a part of the cost
