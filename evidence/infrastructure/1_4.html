Description:

Urdaneta City University (UCU) traces its roots to the vision of Dr. Pedro T. Orata, a renowned educator and Ramon Magsaysay awardee, who aspired to establish a community college that would meet the educational needs of local youth and young adults.

Guided by his principle of “education for all,” UCU has grown into a transformative institution that continues to shape lives and communities across Pangasinan.

From its humble beginnings, UCU has developed into a pioneering local university that upholds academic excellence while championing sustainable development.

Central to this mission is its commitment to building and maintaining a green campus—an environment where learning and environmental stewardship go hand in hand.

The campus reflects this vision through its eco-friendly facilities and practices.

The grounds are enriched with native trees and plants, fostering biodiversity and creating a peaceful atmosphere conducive to learning.

Organic and sustainable gardening areas not only support food sustainability initiatives but also serve as practical learning spaces for students.

UCU also implements innovative resource management strategies. Solar panels and energy-efficient lighting systems reduce its carbon footprint while providing real-world examples for students in environmental science and engineering.

Rainwater harvesting systems are likewise in place, conserving water and minimizing dependence on municipal supplies.

Beyond infrastructure, UCU cultivates a culture of sustainability among its stakeholders.

The university regularly conducts environmental programs such as tree planting activities, campus clean-up drives, and awareness seminars, instilling in students the values of ecological responsibility and community involvement.

This commitment is further embedded in the curriculum, where sustainability principles are integrated across disciplines.

By equipping graduates with the knowledge and skills to address environmental challenges, UCU ensures that its educational mission contributes not only to individual growth but also to global sustainability goals.

Through its green initiatives, Urdaneta City University enriches the academic journey of its students while serving as a model for other institutions in the region.

By harmonizing academic rigor with environmental responsibility, UCU exemplifies how education can be a powerful force for positive change in both people and communities.
