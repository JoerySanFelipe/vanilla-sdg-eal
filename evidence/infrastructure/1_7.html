Urdaneta City University (UCU) provides a well-structured campus setting with facilities that support learning, research, student services, and community engagement. Its academic buildings include specialized spaces for health sciences, criminology, pharmacy, information technology, and business, alongside libraries, laboratories, and resource centers.

The campus also integrates wellness and safety through its clinics, fitness gyms, gymnasium, Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office, and accessible common areas. Administrative and multipurpose buildings further ensure smooth university operations and opportunities for student leadership and development.

Together, these facilities reflect UCU’s commitment to providing a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment for both academic and personal growth.

Turnstile / Facade Building - 64 sq.m

The Turnstile/Facade Building, occupying 64 square meters, serves as a modern gateway to Urdaneta City University. It was strategically designed to strengthen campus security while ensuring smooth and efficient access for students, faculty, and visitors. At its core is an automated turnstile system that regulates entry, allowing only authorized individuals while minimizing congestion during peak hours. Its contemporary design—marked by sleek lines, durable materials, and large windows that maximize natural lighting—creates a professional yet welcoming atmosphere. Inside, an information kiosk assists guests by providing maps, directions, and campus details, improving the visitor experience. Accessibility is thoughtfully integrated, with wide entryways and clear signage that accommodate individuals with disabilities. Security cameras and monitoring systems further enhance safety, allowing personnel to promptly address concerns. Overall, this building is more than just an entry point—it is a symbol of UCU’s commitment to both security and hospitality, seamlessly blending function, safety, and aesthetics.
