Urdaneta City University (UCU) demonstrates a forward-thinking approach to campus planning by dedicating a significant portion of its grounds to open space—an essential element in fostering a vibrant, healthy, and conducive learning environment. The university spans a total land area of 35,544 square meters, of which 21,731 square meters are designated as open space. This figure is derived by subtracting the combined area of campus buildings (8,947 square meters) and forest vegetation (4,866 square meters) from the total campus area, resulting in open spaces that comprise approximately 61.1% of the entire campus.

This substantial proportion of open space highlights UCU’s strong commitment to sustainability, environmental stewardship, and student well-being. The expansive open areas function not only as places for recreation and relaxation but also as vital spaces that contribute to ecological balance and environmental preservation within the university grounds. These areas help regulate the campus microclimate, support biodiversity, and provide natural surroundings that enhance the overall campus atmosphere.

Moreover, these open spaces strengthen the sense of community within the university. They serve as gathering points for students, faculty, and staff—encouraging interaction, collaboration, and outdoor learning opportunities. The presence of these green and accessible areas also enhances the campus’s aesthetic appeal, creating a welcoming and inspiring setting for academic pursuits.

Through this balanced integration of infrastructure and open landscapes, Urdaneta City University reinforces its vision of a sustainable and people-centered campus—one that nurtures academic excellence while promoting environmental responsibility, wellness, and a strong sense of community.
