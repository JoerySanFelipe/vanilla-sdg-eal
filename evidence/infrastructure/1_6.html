[1] Setting and Infrastructure (SI)

[1.5] Total Campus Area (meter²)

The campus of Urdaneta City University (UCU) covers an impressive total area of 35,544 square meters, thoughtfully designed to serve as both an academic hub and a community-oriented environment.

This expansive space reflects a careful balance between natural landscapes, educational facilities, and essential amenities, making it an ideal setting for learning, collaboration, and holistic growth.

At the heart of its design is the integration of green and open spaces, with landscaped gardens, forested areas, and parks that provide a refreshing backdrop to academic life.

These natural features not only enhance the campus’s aesthetic charm but also promote relaxation, wellness, and recreational activities, encouraging students and faculty to embrace a healthy and active lifestyle.

Complementing these green areas are UCU’s modern academic and institutional infrastructures, strategically built to support a wide range of programs and disciplines.

The campus includes well-equipped classrooms, advanced laboratories, and specialized buildings such as the Engineering Management and Auxiliary Services Building, the Sports Development Building, and the Nursing Buildings.

Each facility is designed to address the specific needs of students and faculty, fostering an environment that supports innovation, hands-on learning, and professional preparation.

Beyond academics, UCU also provides a wealth of essential amenities that enhance campus life.

Libraries serve as knowledge hubs, while the gymnasium, dining facilities, and student-centered spaces support physical, social, and cultural development.

The university further enriches its environment through recreational areas such as the Botanical Garden, Chocolate Park, and Orata Park, which serve as venues for outdoor learning, community gatherings, and personal reflection.

Overall, Urdaneta City University’s expansive grounds exemplify a holistic approach to campus design—one that integrates modern infrastructure with the serenity of nature.

By harmonizing educational spaces with recreational and ecological features, UCU not only cultivates academic excellence but also nurtures a strong sense of well-being, belonging, and community among its members.
