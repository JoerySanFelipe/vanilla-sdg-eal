At Urdaneta City University (UCU), our energy consumption data reflects a clear and measurable pattern of usage and efficiency across the years. Based on converted estimates from electricity costs, the campus recorded an average annual consumption ranging from approximately 378,686 kWh in 2020 to a peak of about 1,023,720 kWh in 2022, before stabilizing at around 942,636 kWh in 2023 and an estimated 978,113 kWh in 2025.

This trend highlights the evolving energy demand of the university alongside ongoing efforts toward better energy management. While consumption fluctuates across months—often peaking during mid-year periods—the data provides a strong baseline for evaluating operational efficiency and planning future sustainability initiatives.

With the integration of a 4.5 kW solar panel system, the university strengthens its commitment to reducing reliance on conventional energy sources. This combination of grid electricity and renewable solar input represents a practical step toward a more balanced and sustainable energy model.

Overall, these figures do not only represent kilowatt-hours consumed—they serve as a foundation for informed decision-making, improved energy efficiency, and a continued commitment to environmental stewardship and long-term sustainability.
