A Comprehensive framework for the Energy and Climate Change Programs Utilizing ICT at Urdaneta City University, covering planning, implementation, monitoring, and evaluation:

1. Planning Framework
Goal To promote sustainable energy use and climate change awareness at Urdaneta City University by leveraging ICT for efficient energy management, data-driven decision-making, and educational outreach.
Objectives
  • Implement ICT systems to monitor and manage energy consumption across campus facilities.
  • Increase awareness and engagement in climate action initiatives among students and faculty.
  • Reduce the university’s carbon footprint through targeted energy reduction strategies.
  • Integrate sustainability practices into the university’s curriculum and operations.
Activities
  • Conduct a campus-wide energy audit to identify areas of high energy use and inefficiency.
  • Identify and select suitable ICT tools for energy monitoring, such as IoT-enabled energy meters and data management software.
  • Develop a budget and timeline for implementing ICT-based energy solutions.
  • Collaborate with sustainability experts and potential technology partners for resources and expertise.
Stakeholders University administration, facilities management, IT department, faculty members, students, local environmental agencies, and technology partners.
2. Implementation Phases
Phase 1 Energy Monitoring and Management
  • Install IoT-based energy meters and sensors in key areas (e.g., lecture halls, libraries, laboratories, dormitories) to gather real-time data on energy consumption.
  • Integrate energy management software for data analysis, allowing facilities management to monitor energy use patterns and make adjustments.
  • Set up an automated system for lighting, HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning), and other utilities to minimize energy waste.
Phase 2 Data-Driven Climate Action Initiatives
  • Develop a dashboard displaying real-time energy usage data, accessible to students and faculty, to raise awareness about campus energy consumption.
  • Implement a mobile app or web-based platform where students and faculty can view energy-saving tips, carbon footprint data, and participate in sustainability challenges.
  • Conduct training workshops for staff on using ICT tools for efficient resource management and carbon reduction.
Phase 3 Green Campus Education and Engagement
  • Create an online module or interactive resources on energy conservation and climate change for integration into academic courses.
  • Launch digital campaigns (e.g., social media, email newsletters) to promote sustainability practices and share climate-related updates.
  • Implement a “Green Points” system where students and faculty earn points for engaging in sustainable activities, tracked through the ICT platform.
Phase 4 Renewable Energy Integration
  • Install solar panels or other renewable energy sources where feasible, connected to the ICT system for monitoring renewable energy production and usage.
  • Set up a virtual dashboard to showcase the contribution of renewable energy on campus, inspiring further climate action.
Timeline Define phases for each implementation step, ideally over a 1-3 years period, allowing for gradual integration with current infrastructure and ensuring smooth transitions.
3. Monitoring Framework & KPIs
KPIs
  • Energy Consumption: Track the reduction in electricity usage across campus buildings after implementation.
  • Carbon Footprint: Monitor the decrease in the university’s carbon emissions based on energy-saving initiatives.
  • User Engagement: Measure the number of users accessing the sustainability dashboard, app engagement rates, and participation in Green Points activities.
  • Cost Savings: Calculate monthly or annual savings in utility bills due to ICT-enabled energy management.
  • Renewable Energy Contribution: Track the percentage of campus energy needs met through renewable sources (e.g., solar power).
Activities
  • Monthly reports on energy consumption and cost savings, including feedback from facilities management on areas of improvement.
  • Collect user data and feedback on the sustainability dashboard and mobile app engagement.
  • Conduct periodic surveys to gather insights from students, faculty, and staff on climate awareness and program satisfaction.
  • Report the impact of renewable energy systems, if installed, to showcase benefits in terms of emission reduction and cost savings.
Personnel The IT department for dashboard and app management, facilities management for energy data tracking, and sustainability coordinators for program engagement.
4. Evaluation & Reporting Framework
Criteria
  • Effectiveness: Assess if the ICT tools achieved significant energy savings and improved climate awareness.
  • Efficiency: Evaluate if the savings in energy costs offset the costs of implementing and maintaining ICT systems.
  • Engagement: Analyze engagement metrics to determine if students and staff are actively participating in sustainability initiatives.
  • Sustainability: Determine if the ICT-based energy management practices can be maintained and scaled in the long term.
Activities
  • End-of-year impact assessment to evaluate outcomes against goals and objectives.
  • Compare baseline energy consumption and emissions data with post-implementation figures to determine quantitative improvements.
  • Conduct focus groups or feedback sessions with students and staff to assess satisfaction and gather suggestions.
  • Use the insights gathered to refine the program, identify areas for future improvement, and consider scaling efforts.
Reporting
  • Annual Sustainability Report: Complete brief of achievements, challenges, and recommendations for ongoing and future energy and climate programs.
  • Stakeholder Presentation: Present findings to university administration and relevant stakeholders, sharing success stories and best practices with other educational institutions interested in sustainable ICT solutions.
