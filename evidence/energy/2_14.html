Urdaneta City University (UCU) organizes impactful community and campus-wide programs targeting climate action, environmental resource efficiency, and sustainable waste mitigation.

1. Regional Climate Change Program: "Green Horizons Initiative"
Scope Regional
Participants 150
Description The "Green Horizons Initiative" aims to address climate change impacts within the region surrounding Urdaneta City University through collaborative efforts in education, community engagement, and sustainable practices. This program will foster a culture of environmental stewardship, empower local communities, and promote resilience against climate-related challenges.
2. WAR ON WASTE
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Description The War on Waste program is an initiative focused on minimizing waste generation and maximizing resource recovery to support energy efficiency and mitigate climate change impacts. This program tackles waste at multiple levels, from reducing consumption and promoting reuse to recycling and waste-to-energy solutions. By adopting a circular economy approach, the War on Waste reduces landfill dependency, limits greenhouse gas emissions, and conserves natural resources, all of which contribute to energy savings and a lower carbon footprint.
3. Waste Reduction and Resource Efficiency
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Objective Minimize waste through mindful consumption and reduce demand on natural resources.
Activities Implementing “Zero Waste” policies, encouraging eco-friendly practices on campus, and promoting reusable products over single-use items.
Impact Reduces energy use in waste processing and curbs emissions associated with producing and disposing of products.
4. Recycling and Upcycling Initiatives
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Objective Increase recycling rates and give new life to discarded materials, reducing energy demands of raw material extraction and processing.
Activities Set up recycling stations for plastic, paper, metal, and electronics; workshops on upcycling and creative reuse projects.
Impact Cuts down on energy-intensive manufacturing processes, conserves resources, and diverts waste from landfills.
5. Composting and Organic Waste Management
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Objective Reduce food waste and organic materials sent to landfills by creating compost for campus landscaping and green spaces.
Activities On-campus composting facilities and educational programs on composting techniques.
Impact Decreases methane emissions from landfills, enriches soil health, and supports carbon sequestration efforts.
6. Waste-to-Energy Solutions
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Objective Convert non-recyclable waste into energy to reduce landfill waste and create a sustainable energy source.
Activities Feasibility studies on small-scale waste-to-energy technology, partnerships with waste management companies, and pilot programs for bioenergy from organic waste.
Impact Provides renewable energy, reduces reliance on fossil fuels, and minimizes landfill-related emissions.
7. Community Awareness and Education Campaigns
Scope University Wide
Participants 14,600
Objective Foster a community culture of sustainability and responsibility in waste management.
Activities Workshops, seminars, and awareness campaigns that educate students and the community on reducing, reusing, and recycling.
Impact Empowers individuals to make sustainable choices that collectively contribute to energy savings and climate action.

The War on Waste aligns with broader energy and climate change goals by reducing emissions, conserving energy, and encouraging sustainable resource use, ultimately leading to a cleaner, more resilient environment.
