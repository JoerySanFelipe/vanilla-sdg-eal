Innovative Energy and Climate Change Programs for Urdaneta City University (UCU):

1. Green Campus Initiative (UCU Sustainable Transformation Program)
Objective To transform UCU into a carbon-conscious and energy-efficient university model in Northern Luzon by integrating renewable energy systems, sustainable infrastructure, and eco-friendly campus operations.
Activities
  • A. Renewable Energy Integration
    • Installation of solar photovoltaic (PV) panels on rooftops of major buildings (library, administration, and classrooms)
    • Deployment of solar-powered streetlights across campus
    • Energy monitoring systems to track electricity consumption per building
  • B. Waste Reduction and Management
    • Strict implementation of segregated waste bins (biodegradable, recyclable, residual)
    • Establishment of a Material Recovery Facility (MRF) inside campus
    • “Zero Plastic Week” campaigns every semester
  • C. Sustainable Transport System
    • Introduction of bicycle lanes and bike-sharing stations
    • Promotion of electric shuttle service for internal campus transport
    • Incentives for students who use low-carbon transport
  • D. Green Infrastructure
    • Expansion of urban gardens, vertical gardens, and tree-planting zones
    • Development of climate-resilient landscaping (native plant species)
    • Installation of rainwater harvesting systems for irrigation and cleaning
Outcomes
  • 20–40% reduction in campus electricity consumption
  • Significant decrease in solid waste generation
  • Improved air quality and thermal comfort
  • Hands-on sustainability learning environment for students
  • Certification pathway toward a “Green University Status”
2. Energy Innovation and Research Laboratory (UCU Clean Energy Hub)
Objective To establish a research-driven innovation hub where students, faculty, and industry partners collaborate on renewable energy technologies and climate solutions.
Activities
  • A. Research & Development Projects
    • Solar energy optimization systems (efficiency studies)
    • Small-scale wind turbine prototypes
    • Bioenergy research using agricultural waste (rice husks, corn husks)
    • Battery storage and energy efficiency experimentation
  • B. Academic Integration
    • Inclusion of lab outputs in engineering, education, and IT curricula
    • Thesis and capstone project incubation program
    • Interdisciplinary collaboration across departments
  • C. Skills Training & Workshops
    • Hands-on training in solar panel installation and maintenance
    • Energy auditing workshops for buildings
    • Data analytics for energy consumption monitoring
  • D. Industry Collaboration
    • Partnerships with:
      • DOE (Department of Energy)
      • DOST (Department of Science and Technology)
      • Local renewable energy companies
    • Internship pipelines for students in clean energy sectors
Outcomes
  • Development of functional renewable energy prototypes
  • Increased student research publications in sustainability
  • Establishment of UCU as a regional clean energy innovation center
  • Improved employability of graduates in green industries
3. Climate Change Education and Advocacy Program (UCU Climate Action Initiative)
Objective To strengthen climate literacy and empower students and the community to actively participate in climate action and environmental protection.
Activities
  • A. Climate Education Integration
    • Climate change modules embedded in general education subjects
    • Faculty training on environmental and sustainability teaching approaches
  • B. Seminars and Awareness Campaigns
    • Quarterly climate forums featuring experts from:
      • PAGASA
      • DENR
      • Environmental NGOs
    • Campus-wide “Climate Week” with exhibits and competitions
  • C. Community Outreach Programs
    • Tree planting in nearby barangays
    • Coastal and river clean-up drives
    • Climate awareness caravans in public schools
  • D. Digital Advocacy Campaign
    • Student-led social media campaigns on sustainability
    • Creation of UCU Climate Action online platform
    • Short video competitions promoting eco-friendly behavior
Outcomes
  • Increased climate awareness among students and community
  • Stronger university-community environmental partnerships
  • Behavioral shift toward sustainable practices
  • Student leadership in environmental advocacy
4. Eco-Innovation Challenge (UCU Green Solutions Competition)
Objective To encourage innovation and entrepreneurship among students by developing practical solutions for energy efficiency and climate resilience.
Activities
  • A. Annual Innovation Competition
    • Categories:
      • Renewable energy solutions
      • Waste management innovation
      • Climate adaptation technologies
      • Smart agriculture systems
  • B. Mentorship Program
    • Guidance from faculty and industry experts
    • Startup incubation support for winning projects
  • C. Prototype Development Support
    • Seed funding for top innovations
    • Access to Energy Innovation Lab facilities
    • Business model development workshops
  • D. Exhibition and Demo Day
    • Public presentation of student innovations
    • Investment pitching opportunities
Outcomes
  • Generation of patentable and scalable green technologies
  • Development of student startups in sustainability
  • Increased entrepreneurial mindset in climate solutions
  • Strengthened innovation culture within UCU
5. Sustainable Agriculture and Climate-Resilient Energy Program (Agro-Energy Integration Initiative)
Objective To integrate renewable energy systems with sustainable agricultural practices, addressing both food security and climate change adaptation in local communities.
Activities
  • A. Smart Agriculture Systems
    • Solar-powered irrigation systems
    • Sensor-based soil moisture and climate monitoring
    • Drone-based crop monitoring (pilot projects)
  • B. Climate-Resilient Farming Techniques
    • Organic farming and composting workshops
    • Crop diversification strategies
    • Water conservation and rainwater irrigation systems
  • C. Agro-Energy Research Projects
    • Use of agricultural waste for bioenergy production
    • Hybrid solar-agriculture demonstration farms
    • Energy-efficient greenhouse systems
  • D. Community Extension Programs
    • Training farmers in nearby municipalities
    • Collaboration with DA (Department of Agriculture)
    • Demonstration farms in partner barangays
Outcomes
  • Increased agricultural productivity with lower environmental impact
  • Adoption of renewable energy in farming communities
  • Strengthened university extension services
  • Contribution to regional food security and climate resilience
