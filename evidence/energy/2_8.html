Urdaneta City University demonstrates its commitment to clean energy and sustainability through a 4.5 kW solar panel system installed in 2017 on the Dr. Teofidez E. Calvero Building. This system generates approximately 4,297 kWh of electricity annually, accounting for about 0.75% of the university’s total yearly energy consumption.

While modest in scale, the solar installation serves as a symbol of UCU’s proactive approach to integrating renewable energy into campus operations. It contributes to reducing carbon emissions, lowering energy costs, and promoting environmentally responsible practices among students, faculty, and staff. The system also demonstrates how targeted renewable energy solutions, even at a small scale, can have measurable impacts on campus sustainability.

Through initiatives like this, UCU continues to lead by example, fostering a culture of innovation, environmental stewardship, and energy consciousness that supports a greener and more sustainable future for the university and its community.
