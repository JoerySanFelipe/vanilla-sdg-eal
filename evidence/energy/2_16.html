Overall Impact

The Energy and Climate Change Programs Utilizing ICT at Urdaneta City University demonstrate Very High Impact, supporting 17 Sustainable Development Goals through smart energy management, renewable energy integration, climate action, digital innovation, education, research, governance, and strategic partnerships.
